# Changelog

## 16001-3.0

- New dungeons: The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, The Shaper's Terrace.
- New keys: Dalaran Sewer Key, Tome of Dalaran.
- Blackrock Spire split into Lower and Upper.
- Chains include lead-in and follow-up quests.
- Step details panel: objectives, 3D model, map pin, Wowhead link.
- Objectives listed under each step.
- Rewards and loot pop-up.
- Search box.
- Options: window size, show other classes.
- Steps can be marked as done.
- Every chain opens as a graph.
- Step marks are the game's quest icons.
- List sorted by level, class chains grouped by class.
- Quest data from QuestieDB 1.1.0.

## 16001-2.1

- The addon has an icon in the game's addon list.
- New button at the minimap: a click opens or closes the window, a drag moves the button. `/gr minimap` hides it or brings it back.
- Versions are now numbered as client version, then major.minor.
